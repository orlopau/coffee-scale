The scale knows a few recipes for filter coffee and walks you through them: how much coffee, how much water, and when to pour.

| Recipe | By |
|---|---|
| Aeropress J.H. | James Hoffmann, "The Ultimate Aeropress Technique" |
| French Press J.H. | James Hoffmann, "The Ultimate French Press Technique" |
| V60 Kasuya | Tetsu Kasuya, the 4:6 method in five pours |
| V60 Scott Rao | Scott Rao |
| V60 Hario Original | Hario's original V60 recipe |

In every step, **click** goes on to the next one and **long-press** goes back one. Long-press in the list of recipes to leave for another mode.

{{% steps %}}

### Choose a recipe

Open **Recipes** from the list of modes. **Turn** to a recipe and **click**.

{{< screen name="recipe_switcher_middle" caption="Select a recipe." >}}

### Read what it's about

A short description of the recipe. Scan the QR code with your phone to open the original recipe.

{{< screen name="recipe_summary_0" caption="The recipe's description and a link to it" >}}

### Set the amount

**Turn** to change the amount of coffee; the water follows. The value blinks while it's the recipe's own.

{{< screen name="recipe_weight_adjusted_up" caption="17.0 g of coffee for 283 ml of water" >}}

### Set the ratio

**Turn** to change how much water per gram of coffee. All pours change with it.

{{< screen name="recipe_ratio_adjusted" caption="1 : 19.0" >}}

### Weigh the coffee

Put your brewer or a cup on the scale, **turn** the knob a little to tare, and add coffee until the scale shows the amount.

{{< screen name="recipe_prepare_some" caption="5.68 g of 12.0 g" >}}

### Brew

**Click** when you start pouring. For each pour, the scale shows what to do, the water still to pour and the time left. **Turn** to tare if you need to.

{{< screen name="recipe_brewing_3_pour_1" caption="Pour: 326.20 g of water still to go, 20 seconds left" >}}

When a pour's time is up, the scale beeps. Depending on the recipe, it goes on to the next pour by itself or waits for your **click**; a click also goes on earlier. `T-` counts down the time to pour, `TP-` a time to wait, e.g. while the coffee blooms. The bar on top shows which pour you're at.

### Done

After the last pour the scale says **Done!**. Click to go back to the list of recipes.
