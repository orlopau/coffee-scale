The scale mode is where the scale starts. It shows the weight on top and a timer below.

{{< screen name="scale_running" caption="12.34 g, and the timer at 1 minute 5.4 seconds" >}}

## Taring

Taring sets the weight on the scale to zero, e.g. after putting a cup on it. There are three ways:

- **Turn** the knob a little.
- **Lift to tare**: slide a finger up the side of the platform, lifting it a little, and let go. The scale beeps twice and tares. This works with a cup on it, too.
- **Auto-tare**: the scale tares by itself when you put on a container whose weight you saved in the settings, see below.

## The timer

**Click** to start the timer, click again to stop it. The next click starts it again from zero.

## Lift to tare

Lifting the platform for a moment can't happen by accident: a cup set down, a tap on the platform or pouring don't do it. How firmly you have to lift is a setting, **Lift-to-Tare**: **Off**, **Gentle**, **Normal** (the default) or **Firm**. If the scale tares when you didn't want it to, choose a firmer level; if it doesn't notice your lifts, a gentler one.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Lift to tare is off while the timer runs: in the scale mode, during a shot and while a recipe brews. So lifting the cup to look at it doesn't zero what you measure.
{{< /callout >}}

## Auto-tare

If you always use the same few cups or containers, save their weights in the settings, under **Auto-Tare Weight 1** to **Auto-Tare Weight 5**. When you put one of them on the scale in the scale mode, it beeps and tares by itself. **Auto-Tare Tolerance** sets how close the weight must be to a saved one.

## Zero tracking

When the scale is empty and steady, it slowly pulls small deviations around zero back to zero, e.g. while it warms up. That way it stays at 0.00 g without taring. It pauses while the timer runs, so the first slow drops of a shot aren't pulled away. The setting **Zero Tracking** sets how far from zero it still does that; 0 turns it off.

## Shot detection

With shot detection on, the scale mode notices when an espresso starts flowing into a tared cup and switches to the espresso screen by itself. See [Espresso](../espresso/#shot-detection).
